Return / Refund Policy

Last Updated: March 23, 2026

1. Overview

If something isn’t right with your TrooperGear order, we’ll help you make it right. This policy explains how returns, exchanges, and refunds work.

2. Return Eligibility

  • Time window: Request a return within 14 days of delivery.
  • Condition: Items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags/labels intact.
  • Proof of purchase: Your order number or email receipt is required.

3. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or you received the wrong item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We may offer a replacement, exchange, or refund depending on the situation.

4. How to Start a Return

  1. Email us at troopergear876@gmail.com with your order number.
  2. Tell us what you’d like: return, exchange, or refund.
  3. We’ll provide return instructions and the next steps.

5. Refunds

  • Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we’ll confirm approval by email.
  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method where possible.
  • Processing times vary by payment provider/bank and may take several business days.

6. Return Shipping

Return shipping costs may apply unless the return is due to a damaged/defective/incorrect item. Any shipping fees charged at checkout are typically non-refundable unless required by applicable law.

7. Policy Notes

  • Some items may be ineligible for return for hygiene and safety reasons.
  • This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws.
